The Ideas Behind My Writing

The Silent Crisis We Cannot Afford to Ignore

We are living in a world that is increasingly connected, yet many people feel emotionally disconnected. Behind achievements, social media smiles, academic pressure, workplace expectations, and the constant need to keep moving, countless individuals struggle silently.

Mental health and emotional well-being are no longer conversations we can postpone. We need to talk about loneliness, anxiety, emotional exhaustion, relationships, identity, and the pressure to appear strong even when life feels overwhelming.

Through my writing, I aim to explore these realities with honesty, empathy, and a deeply human perspective. My upcoming books will examine the emotions we suppress, the experiences that shape us, and the conversations we need to start having with ourselves and others.

Sometimes, a story cannot solve every problem. But it can help someone feel understood enough to begin a conversation.

Let’s make space for the conversations that matter.

Meet Dr. Rehaman Mohammed

Dr Rehaman Mohammed is an award-winning author, recognised with the Emily Dickinson Award for The Soul’s Canvas and DevElet honours for excellence in digital education and transformative leadership.

An educator, researcher, storyteller, and podcast host, he explores human emotions, relationships, nostalgia, social contradictions, and the unspoken realities of everyday life.

Through his books, podcasts, and RehamanUnplugged, he brings literature, contemporary social issues, mental health awareness, and emotional well-being into meaningful conversations.

His books include The Soul’s Canvas, In the Places We Never Left and In Between We Felt.
